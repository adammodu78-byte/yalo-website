Pay with USDT.

Submit a manual USDT payment on Ethereum (ERC20). The customer sends the YALO platform fee and the service-provider amount as two separate transfers, then submits both transaction hashes for review.

Network warning: Send USDT using Ethereum Mainnet (ERC20) only. Do not send USDT on TRON, BNB Chain, Base or another network to these instructions. You must also pay Ethereum gas fees from your wallet.

Submitting this form does not mean your payment is approved. YALO must verify both transactions on Etherscan before confirming the payment or delivering the service. Never share your seed phrase or private key.
